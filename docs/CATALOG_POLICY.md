# Catalog policy

The phase-one catalog promotes legitimate games, wallet codes, and subscriptions. It does not list products that require selling, sharing, renting, or transferring customer account credentials.

In particular, “primary” and “secondary” PlayStation accounts are not seeded as products. PlayStation’s current terms prohibit sharing, buying, selling, renting, sublicensing, trading, or transferring accounts and account credentials. The store can still offer supported alternatives such as full games, PlayStation Store wallet codes, and PlayStation Plus subscriptions.

Before publishing a catalog item, confirm:

- the item is sourced through a legitimate channel;
- the activation region and platform are stated accurately;
- any advertised price is current;
- subscription terms and duration are clear;
- the listing does not require access to a customer’s credentials.

Reference: [PlayStation Network Terms of Service](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/legal/psn-terms-of-service.html/).
