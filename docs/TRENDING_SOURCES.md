# Launch catalog sources

Last reviewed: August 30, 2026.

The launch catalog uses current interest signals as a starting point, not a permanent ranking.

- [PlayStation Store: July 2026 top downloads](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/08/10/playstation-store-july-2026s-top-downloads/) lists EA SPORTS FC 26, Palworld, and Minecraft among leading PS5 downloads in major regions.
- [PlayStation Store: July 2026 top downloads in Asia](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/08/17/20260818-top/) places EA SPORTS FC 26 first and Palworld fourth on the Asia PS5 chart.
- [Steam weekly top sellers, August 18–25, 2026](https://store.steampowered.com/charts/topsellers/US/2026-8-18/) includes HELLDIVERS 2 in the top ten by revenue.
- [PlayStation Plus](https://www.playstation.com/en-ph/support/subscriptions/playstation-plus-support/) confirms the Essential, Extra, and Deluxe membership structure used by the subscription listing.

Review the catalog monthly before changing “trending” items. Availability, price, supported platform, activation region, and product naming must be confirmed separately; popularity charts do not confirm that the store can supply an item.
