Six pages are available in English and Arabic at `/{locale}/{slug}`:

- `about`

The two Terms and Conditions links on the reference website represent language versions and are combined into one bilingual page. All pages are linked in the footer and store-information navigation. Terms and warranty link to Immortal's own primary policy post.

## Primary source

[Immortal Games Store's policy post](https://www.facebook.com/Immortal.G.Store/posts/pfbid02HLV924rePat3EbHEa1MP7FU43tWwTAe9jdmu7g9THobkrdTNXbdwHAMSeHwYXSe7l)

Facebook fetch was throttled; the owner supplied the full Arabic text in this conversation on 15 October 2026. That supplied text is the authoritative source for account terms, with an English translation and readable sectioning.

The specific coverage in that source supersedes the owner's earlier blanket lifetime-warranty statement: store-supplied shared game accounts have five years of account coverage; shared Plus accounts have coverage for the purchased term; third-party accounts have one year of login/logout support without ban or Sony-problem coverage; full accounts have no store warranty after receipt. Account issues are normally resolved within 12 hours, up to 24 hours. The original-purchase refund exception when no replacement game account is available is retained.

Platform rules still apply to transfers and resale. The owner's general policy-update and no-refund language is qualified by applicable consumer rights. Existing product seeding is not changed by this content update.
